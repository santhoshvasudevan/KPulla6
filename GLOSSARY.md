# KPulla6 portfolio reporting

## Language

**Provider category**:
The fund category reported by the NAV provider for a scheme. A provider subcategory is the more specific grouping within that category.

**Portfolio asset class**:
The classification used for exposure totals and filters such as Equity and Debt. It is independent of the category supplied by the fund provider.

**Confirmed FD interest**:
Interest recorded as paid or reinvested for the fixed deposit term that earned it, including a delayed payout once confirmed. Expected or pending amounts and reversed payments are excluded from actual interest totals.
